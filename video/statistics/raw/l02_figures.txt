Running example: 100 students
bus      bus  no bus  All
bike                     
bike      15      45   60
no bike   30      10   40
All       45      55  100
  P(bus)=0.45  P(bike)=0.60
  P(bus|bike)=0.25  P(bus|no bike)=0.75
  independence check: P(bike)*P(bus)*n = 27  vs observed 15
